# Ranking Roni Paul for category searches

## How this works, honestly

A personal site ranks for a person's name within weeks. Ranking for a category phrase such as "health-tech entrepreneur" is a different game: Google decides those results on the strength of pages that are *about the topic*, on links from other sites that use those words, and on how often the name and the phrase appear together across the web. One profile page cannot do that alone, however good its metadata. The levers, in order of weight:

1. **Pages about the topic.** Essays and explainers where the keyword is the natural subject, not a label stuck on the bio.
2. **Mentions elsewhere.** Press, podcasts, directories and lists that describe Roni with the same phrase and link to ronipaul.com.
3. **Entity consistency.** The same descriptor on LinkedIn, Instagram, Wikidata, Crunchbase and the site, so search engines and AI assistants treat "Roni Paul" and "health-tech entrepreneur" as one fact.
4. **On-page signals.** Title, headings, schema and internal links, already in place and tuned below.

Two cautions on the list you gave. "Healthcare" on its own is a dictionary word fought over by hospitals and governments; nobody ranks a personal site for it, so it is used here only as a modifier. "Best medtech entrepreneur" is a superlative: Google answers it with lists and awards, not profiles, so the route to it is appearing on lists, which is a PR task, not a tag.

## Changes to make on ronipaul.com now

- **Widen the descriptor.** Keep "health-tech entrepreneur" as the primary phrase, and add "med-tech" and "healthcare" as natural secondaries: in the About lead ("a health-tech and med-tech entrepreneur"), the FAQ "Who is Roni Paul", the long bio, and `knowsAbout` in schema. One sentence each, not stuffing.
- **Claim Assam.** The site says Bengaluru everywhere and never mentions Assam. Add it to the Journey ("raised in Assam, behind the counter of the family pharmacy"), the About, a FAQ ("Where is Roni Paul from?"), and `birthPlace` in the Person schema. This is the cheapest ranking win on the whole list because almost nobody competes for it.
- **Add a visible one-line summary under the hero** in plain words: "Roni Paul is a health-tech entrepreneur from Assam, based in Bengaluru, and the founder of MedMate." That exact sentence is what Google and AI assistants lift into answers.
- **Title tag variant.** "Roni Paul – Health-Tech Entrepreneur, Founder of MedMate | Bengaluru" adds the city without crowding.
- **Essays page titles** should carry the question people type: "The money that dies on the shelf: why Indian pharmacies lose crores to expiry" rather than the bare headline.
- **Internal links with real anchors.** From the About to each essay using the keyword ("pharmacy expiry losses", "quick commerce vs the chemist"), and from essays back to the profile with "health-tech entrepreneur Roni Paul".
- **Four new essays over the next eight weeks**, one per fortnight, from Tier 3: pharmacy count in India; how a chemist goes online without an app; e-prescription rules for Indian clinics; 100 days of building in public. Each gets its own page, Article schema, feed entry and IndexNow ping.

## Off-site work that moves Tier 1 and 2

1. **LinkedIn**: headline done. Next, publish each essay there as an article two days after it goes live on ronipaul.com, with a link back.
2. **Wikidata**: create the item with occupation "entrepreneur", country India, residence Bengaluru, place of birth in Assam, official website ronipaul.com. Google reads this directly.
3. **Crunchbase and Wellfound**: founder profiles for MedMate, bio using the descriptor, link to ronipaul.com.
4. **Directories that rank for the category terms**: F6S, Tracxn (claim the MedMate entry), StartupIndia profile, TiE Bangalore member page, Headstart Network. Each is a page about "Bengaluru health-tech founder" that can link to you.
5. **Press with the right anchor text**: three stories in six months. YourStory or Inc42 founder story; one Assam outlet piece on "Assam-born founder building for India's chemists"; one trade outlet (Pharmabiz, Medical Dialogues, Express Pharma) on the pharmacy-tech angle. Ask each to link "Roni Paul" to ronipaul.com.
6. **Speaking bios**: every event, college and podcast bio uses the same two lines and links the site. Those pages rank for "speaker" and "entrepreneur" queries and compound.
7. **Lists**: nominate for Entrepreneur India 35 Under 35, YourStory Tech30, Inc42 30 Startups to Watch, ET Startup Awards. Being on one list is what wins "best medtech entrepreneur" searches.

## How to measure

- Google Search Console → Performance → Queries, filtered by "entrepreneur", "founder", "Assam", "medtech", monthly. Impressions rise before clicks do.
- Bing Webmaster → Search performance, the same filters.
- Monthly prompt test: ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity "who are notable health-tech entrepreneurs in Bengaluru?" and "who is Roni Paul?" and record whether and what they cite.
- Targets: name queries ranking by week 3; "Assam entrepreneur" and "pharmacy tech founder" impressions by month 2; "health-tech entrepreneur India" top-20 impressions by month 6 if the essays and press land.
